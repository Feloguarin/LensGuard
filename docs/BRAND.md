# LensGuard brand kit

**Name:** LensGuard · **Tagline:** Inspect your space.

LensGuard is a calm, practical companion for examining a room. Its identity connects a lens with a shield, while its language keeps sensor observations separate from conclusions.

## Palette and typography

| Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | `#0D1417` | Primary background |
| Slate | `#182327` | Cards and surfaces |
| Raised slate | `#223136` | Buttons and raised surfaces on cards |
| Lens lime | `#B7F779` | Active controls and identity |
| Soft white | `#EFF5EF` | Main text |
| Muted gray | `#9EB1B5` | Supporting text |
| Amber | `#FFC674` | Limitations and context, never a camera verdict |

Use a clear system sans-serif. The Android app uses the platform font; the SVG wordmark uses Arial/Helvetica/sans-serif fallbacks. Keep body text readable, avoid all-caps paragraphs and preserve contrast on dark backgrounds.

## Identity usage

- Keep the mark's original proportions. Give it clear space of at least one quarter of its width.
- Use the mark at 24 px or larger; use the wordmark when the name needs to be readable.
- Prefer the full banner in its original 2:1 aspect ratio. Avoid crops that remove the title or tagline.
- Show actual screenshots only when captured from the app and caption them with the device/emulator context. Label concept images as illustrations.

## Voice

Use short, specific, evidence-based language: “small highlight,” “magnetic change,” “advertised service,” “inspect from another angle.” Explain what an observation means and what it cannot establish.

Avoid “camera confirmed,” “room safe,” “100% detection,” “RF detector,” “thermal scan,” invented detection probabilities or claims that a clean scan rules out cameras. Avoid depicting a real person's property as secretly recording without evidence.

## Ready-to-use project descriptions

**GitHub description:** Open-source Android room inspection companion: camera, magnetic and nearby wireless clues. Built for Pixel 9; no detection guarantees.

**Short introduction:** LensGuard helps you inspect your space using camera observations, magnetic readings and nearby wireless advertisements. It runs on Android, keeps evidence local until you share it, and is open source under the MIT license.
